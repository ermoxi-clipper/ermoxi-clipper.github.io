Privacy Policy - Selim Clipper

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Selim Clipper is used only by its owner with his own TikTok account.

Data accessed: basic TikTok profile information (user.info.basic) and permission to upload videos as drafts (video.upload).

Storage: Access tokens are stored only locally on the owner's computer. No data is sold, shared, or sent to third parties.

Deletion: Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok settings; local data is deleted on request.
